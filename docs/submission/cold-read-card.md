# Independent cold-read card

Give the reader only the PDF. Observe for 15 minutes without prompting, explaining, or answering in advance. Record the following as observed; do not add identifying details.

1. Page of first frown: ____________________
2. Every question, in the reader's exact words:
   - ____________________________________________________________
   - ____________________________________________________________
   - ____________________________________________________________
   - ____________________________________________________________
3. Where the reader voluntarily stops within 15 minutes, if applicable: ____________________

Decision: pass when the reader asks zero questions. Record one card for each actual session.
